# Chapter 1: The Keeper

The lighthouse stood at the end of the world, or so it seemed to Nadia. Every night she climbed the hundred and twelve steps and lit the lamp.

"Will anyone ever come?" she asked the gulls. They did not answer. They never did.

# Chapter 2: The Ship

On the ninth night of the storm, a light blinked back across the water. Nadia's heart leapt. Someone was out there, and they had seen her.

She ran down the stairs, laughing, and threw open the door to the wind.
